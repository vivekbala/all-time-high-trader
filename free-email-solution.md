## 📧 **Where Do Emails Go?**

**Current setup**: Emails are **logged to browser console** (you can see them in developer tools)

**To collect real emails, you have these FREE options:**

### **Option 1: Browser Console (Current)**
- Emails appear in browser console
- Perfect for testing and small volumes
- No setup required

### **Option 2: Google Forms (Free)**
1. Create a Google Form
2. Get the form URL
3. Update your form action
4. Emails go to Google Sheets

### **Option 3: EmailJS (Free)**
1. Sign up at [emailjs.com](https://emailjs.com) (free)
2. Get API key
3. Update your form
4. Emails go directly to your email

### **Option 4: Netlify Forms (Free)**
1. Connect your GitHub repo to Netlify
2. Change form action to `#`
3. Netlify automatically collects emails
